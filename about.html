OUR HISTORY

The Gold Gym Story

Since 2017, Gold Gym Hetauda has been the premier fitness destination in Hetauda-4, Makwanpur. Founded with a vision to bring world-class gym standards, olympic free weights, and certified coaching to our hometown, we have empowered hundreds of individuals to build confidence and strength.

Whether you are stepping into a gym for the very first time or an experienced athlete preparing for powerlifting and bodybuilding, our welcoming community and expert coaches support your journey every rep of the way.
